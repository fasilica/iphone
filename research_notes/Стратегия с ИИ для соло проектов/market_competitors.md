> Method note for the report writer: research was done on 2026-10-06. Direct page fetching (WebFetch) was blocked by the network egress proxy for almost every domain tried (carta.com, gusto.com, joinhampton.com, inpaceline.com, sostav.ru, secrets.tbank.ru, plan-c-strategy.com, strateg.aipeople.by, aicofounderstack.com). ## 1. AI products positioned as strategy partner / AI co-founder / AI board of advisors / AI business coach

### Takeaway
The "AI co-founder / AI board / AI C-suite" category is crowded and cheap ($0–$120/month). It splits into three groups:
- **Agent "company OS" products** that execute tasks: Cofounder, Sintra.
- **Persona-debate advisory apps**: CofoundAI, BoardroomAI, FoundersOrbit.
- **Free GPTs.**

Enterprise strategy-execution suites (Quantive, Cascade, Strategyzer) are priced and designed for organizations, not solos. Collaboration canvases (Miro, Mural) now ship AI agents and facilitation features but no strategy methodology. The general-purpose assistants (Claude at $20–200/month with Cowork plugins; ChatGPT) are the real default "hire". Recurring complaints across the category are generic advice and sycophancy, meaning the AI agrees with the founder instead of challenging them.

**Complaints and reviews**
- AI coaching gives "generic advice" that lacks pattern recognition from real cohorts. It avoids hard truths: it "won't tell a founder their leadership style is killing retention". Managers reported spending 90 min/week reviewing generic AI suggestions. Caveat: the source is Noomii, a human-coach marketplace with a commercial interest in this conclusion — [Noomii, 2026](https://orgs.noomii.com/the-ai-coaching-experiment-that-backfired/)
- AI assistants "adapt responses to match what they think you want to hear", because they are trained to be agreeable — [The Search Sherpa](https://thesearchsherpa.com/ai-business-decisions-honest-feedback/)
- Solo-founder guide to "what to expect" from an AI business coach — [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/solo-founders-ai-business-coach-what-to-expect/)

### Inferences
- **The "AI board / C-suite persona" pattern is commoditized** ($0–29/month, multiple near-identical apps). Its core feature, multiple personas debating, can be reproduced with one prompt in Claude or ChatGPT.
- **Execution-oriented "AI co-founder" products don't do strategy facilitation.** Cofounder and Sintra are about doing tasks. None of the products found offers a strategy process: diverge/converge, hypotheses, state that persists across sessions, decision records, review cadence.
- **Enterprise strategy suites are mis-sized for solos.** Quantive's median contract is ~$49K/yr, and Cascade and Strategyzer sell seat/enterprise licenses. Strategyzer's $299 AI-prompt program shows methodology owners are starting to sell "method + AI prompts" as a productized package. That is a direct precedent for the user's offer.
- **Sycophancy and generic advice are the main documented complaints.** This supports the user's thesis that a methodology-driven challenger role (human or structured protocol) is valuable. Note that the most vocal sources have a commercial bias.

### Gaps
- No reliable traction or review data (G2/Capterra star ratings, Product Hunt upvotes, MAU) was found for CofoundAI, BoardroomAI, FoundersOrbit, Board or VentureBoard.
- No usage numbers for strategy GPTs in the GPT Store (conversation counts) were found.
- Notion AI 2026 pricing and any strategy-specific Notion templates were not checked (search budget ran out).
- Strategyzer's current plan tiers beyond the Capterra "from $250/user/month" figure were not verified.
- OpenAI/ChatGPT 2026 consumer and business plan prices were not checked directly.

## 2. Open-source / free alternatives (prompt packs, skills repos, templates, guides)

### Takeaway
A large free layer now exists: Anthropic's own open-source Cowork plugins, community skill marketplaces (PM skills with /strategy → /business-model → /pricing chains, JTBD workshop skills), obra/superpowers (whose brainstorming skill is a Socratic dialogue protocol), free Google "AI for Business" courses, and many free YouTube "Claude Code for non-coders" courses. A methodology-based strategy package can be partly copied as free skills, so lasting value must come from facilitation, judgment, accountability and the client's own data and context.

### Cited Findings
- **obra/superpowers** (Jesse Vincent): a "complete software development methodology for coding agents", MIT-licensed.
  - Reportedly **200,000+ GitHub stars as of late June 2026**, with 14 skills and ~3.1M total installs. These figures come from secondary aggregators and were not checked on GitHub — [pasqualepillitteri.it](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/3235/claude-code-skills-daily-workflow-senior-developer-2026); [skillselion guide](https://skillselion.com/guides/claude-code-superpowers-skill-guide)
  - Its **brainstorming** skill is mandatory "before any creative work". It runs three rounds of Socratic questioning: the model asks clarifying questions, proposes alternatives, presents design sections and waits for explicit approval at each phase — [claudemarketplaces.com](https://claudemarketplaces.com/skills/obra/superpowers/brainstorming)
- **phuryn/pm-skills** ("PM Skills Marketplace", by Paweł Huryn / The Product Compass): 9 Claude plugins installable in Cowork. Commands chain: **/strategy → /business-model → /pricing → /plan-launch** — [instagit guide](https://instagit.com/phuryn/pm-skills/how-to-install-pm-skills-marketplace-for-claude-cowork); [The Product Compass](https://www.productcompass.pm/i/188624548)
- **JTBD skills on free marketplaces:**
  - borghei/Claude-Skills "jtbd-workshop" runs full JTBD discovery workshops. It outputs job hierarchies, the forces behind switching, and desired-outcome statements — [claudeskills.info](https://claudeskills.info/skills/borghei/Claude-Skills/jtbd-workshop/)
  - product-on-purpose/pm-skills "define-jtbd-canvas" covers functional, emotional and social dimensions — [claudemarketplaces.com](https://claudemarketplaces.com/skills/product-on-purpose/pm-skills/define-jtbd-canvas)
  - A "jtbd-extractor" skill also exists — [vibeindex.ai](https://www.vibeindex.ai/skills/pratikshadake/claude-product-management-skills/jtbd-extractor)
- **Anthropic's 11 launch Cowork plugins** are open-source — [Reworked](https://www.reworked.co/collaboration-productivity/anthropic-adds-plugins-to-claude-cowork/)
- **Miroverse** (Miro's free template gallery) has thousands of reusable boards, increasingly with AI Flows/Sidekicks built in — [gend.co](https://www.gend.co/blog/new-miroverse-templates-flows-sidekicks)
- **Free learning:**
  - A free 6-hour YouTube course "Claude Code for Non-Coders" — [videohighlight summary](https://videohighlight.com/v/jdbOVepEtUE)
  - Substack newsletters dedicated to Claude Code for non-coders — [claudecodefornoncoders.substack.com](https://claudecodefornoncoders.substack.com); [Nate's Newsletter](https://natesnewsletter.substack.com/p/claude-code-without-the-code-the)
  - Google's free practical "ИИ для бизнеса" course in Russian/Ukrainian. The 100 most active participants get mentoring to build their own AI strategy — [ITC.ua](https://itc.ua/articles/otkryta-regystratsyya-na-besplatnuyu-programmu-yy-dlya-byznesa-ot-google-obzor-programmy-y-formatov/); [Ekonomicheskaya Pravda](https://epravda.com.ua/rus/tehnologiji/ii-dlya-biznesa-google-zapuskaet-besplatnuyu-prakticheskuyu-programmu-dlya-predprinimateley-813517/)
- **Free GPTs:** Startup Mentor and CEO GPT (see section 1) — [firmbee](https://firmbee.com/gpt-store-the-most-interesting-gpts)

### Inferences
- Free skill packs already cover the "frameworks" layer: strategy, business model, pricing, JTBD canvases, Socratic brainstorming. A paid offer that is mostly "my framework as a skill" faces commoditization within months.
- Free tools lack the parts the user's thesis depends on:
  - an end-to-end multi-day strategy *process* with a persistent state file;
  - a human facilitator who challenges assumptions and holds the founder accountable;
  - integration with the client's real data (CRM, finance);
  - governance and decision protocols for tiny teams, such as Sociocracy 3.0 patterns.
- Publishing a free "lite" skill is likely a good acquisition channel, mirroring how pm-skills and superpowers built audiences.

### Gaps
- **Zamesin's Next Move Theory skill:** search excerpts mention a conversational Claude skill ("nmt-chat") that acts as "a senior product advisor who thinks in Ivan Zamesin's Next Move Theory / Advanced JTBD". It reportedly covers product, strategy, segmentation, value, pricing, growth, retention, positioning and B2B. The exact repository URL, license, star count and pricing could not be confirmed (fetching blocked; follow-up search found nothing).
- No GitHub star counts were verified directly. The superpowers 200K figure is from aggregators.
- No Russian-language open-source strategy skill packs were found. Search budget ran out before a dedicated check.

## 3. Human and hybrid alternatives (facilitators, coaches, fractional execs, peer groups, accelerators) — international and Russia/CIS prices

### Takeaway
Human alternatives are 1–3 orders of magnitude more expensive than AI tools:
- **International:** fractional COO $3–15K/month, EOS implementer $3.5–10K/session-day, Vistage $12–15K/yr, Hampton $8.5K/yr.
- **Russia:** strategic session moderation 49K–950K ₽, median ~140K ₽/day in Moscow; small-business group sessions ~100–150K ₽.

Peer groups mostly gate on revenue (Hampton >$3M; Атланты ~1–1.5B ₽ average turnover; «Твоя Опора» ≥10M ₽), so early solo founders are largely excluded. That leaves a price and access gap between free/$20 AI and $5K+ human help.

### Inferences
- **The price ladder has a gap.** The ladder runs:

  | Option | Price |
  |---|---|
  | AI tools | $0–200/month |
  | Masterminds | ~20–30K ₽/month or €150–300/event |
  | Russian strat session | 70–250K ₽/day |
  | Fractional exec / EOS | $3–15K/month |
  | Elite peer groups (revenue-gated) | $8.5–15K/yr |

  The gap is a **mid-priced, solo-sized, recurring strategy service** (roughly 30–150K ₽ per sprint, or a subscription), sized for one founder or a team of 2–5 rather than an executive team.
- **Russian strategic sessions are built for teams of 5–30**, often paid from HR budgets. A solo founder has no HR budget and pays personally (the 21% "owner's budget" segment), so pricing must fit a personal wallet.
- **Peer groups' "personal board of directors" is the closest human analog** to "AI board of advisors" apps. A hybrid could combine a small peer circle with AI-maintained state files, a "forum group + AI" format.

## 4. Russian-speaking market specifics (who sells стратсессии / AI-for-entrepreneurs / AI strategy; formats; access to Claude/ChatGPT; YandexGPT/GigaChat)

### Takeaway

## 7. White spaces, positioning opportunities, and risks

### Takeaway
The whitespace is a **mid-priced, solo/tiny-team-sized, methodology-led strategy process run inside the client's own AI workspace** (state file, connectors, skills), with a human facilitator for challenge and accountability. Today's market is split between cheap agreeable AI personas, team-sized human strat sessions, revenue-gated peer groups, and automation-focused AI clubs.

The main risks:
- free skill packs commoditizing the framework layer;
- model vendors (Anthropic plugins, Yandex Alice AI Pro with 120+ skills, Miro Sidekicks) absorbing features;
- access and payment friction for Western models in Russia;
- low willingness to pay in the micro segment.

### Inferences
**White spaces**
1. **"Strategy *with* AI" for one founder or a team of 2–5.** Russian strat-session providers price per day for 5–30-person teams, and AI workshops focus on AI adoption. No one sells a 1:1 or tiny-team multi-day strategy sprint inside the client's own Claude/ChatGPT workspace. This is the client's own project/folder with a state file, CRM/finance connectors and a Miro board, which the client keeps and continues using after the facilitator leaves.
2. **Mid-price tier.** A productized sprint priced between masterminds (~20–30K ₽) and team strat sessions (100–250K ₽), plus an optional monthly "strategy review" subscription. Revenue-gated peer groups leave pre-$3M / pre-10M ₽ founders unserved.
3. **Challenge-by-design to counter AI sycophancy.**
   - *Protocol side:* explicit devil's-advocate and red-team roles, S3 consent and objection rounds applied to AI suggestions, hypotheses with kill criteria.
   - *Human side:* a facilitator who holds accountability.
   - This answers the most documented complaint about AI advisors.
4. **Governance for AI-native tiny teams.** No product found addresses *how a 2–5-person team plus AI agents makes and records decisions* (roles, domains, decision logs, review cadence). The user's Sociocracy 3.0 background fits this, and none of the AI co-founder apps cover it.
5. **Russia-specific "AI workspace setup" layer.** Help with access and payment routes, data-localized alternatives (GigaChat/YandexGPT/Alice AI Pro) and Russian-language skills. Note: legality and compliance of reseller routes should be checked before recommending them.
6. **Train-the-facilitator / licensing.** Package the protocol and skills for other Russian-speaking facilitators and coaches, by analogy with Design Sprint Academy's €2,500 AI facilitator training.

**Positioning options (hypotheses)**
- "Your strategy department in a folder: methodology + your AI workspace + a facilitator with no stake in your decisions", or reframed as "skin in the game stays with you; process discipline comes from us".
- "Decision quality, not productivity", using the Stripe 34x→61x dispersion as the hook.
- Use a free "lite" skill or prompt pack and public cases as lead magnets, as AI Practiq, pm-skills and superpowers built audiences. Sell process, accountability, integration and community, not the frameworks themselves.

**Risks**
- **Commoditization:** frameworks (JTBD, BMC, strategy chains) are already free as skills. Any paid value tied to "secret prompts" will erode quickly.
- **Vendor absorption:** Anthropic and OpenAI are adding plugins, memory and connectors. Yandex ships 120+ business skills. Miro and Mural add AI facilitation. Expect "strategy assistant" templates natively inside these platforms within 6–12 months. This is an inference based on the cadence observed, not a vendor announcement.
- **Access risk in Russia:** Claude/ChatGPT accounts can be banned and payments blocked. Client data sitting in Western tools may raise 152-FZ data concerns for some clients.
- **Willingness to pay:** solo founders get only 14.7% of priced-round cash, and Russian solopreneurs are mostly micro, so price sensitivity is high.
- **Proof problem:** "one-person unicorn" narratives are partly hype (the Medvi dispute). Claims of strategic impact will need the user's own case evidence.
- **AI tool churn:** >40% early failure rates suggest clients need onboarding and habit-building support, not just a setup. This is also an opportunity.

### Gaps
- No direct customer-interview or survey data on willingness to pay for AI-augmented strategy facilitation (Russian or international) was found.
- No evidence was found of incumbents (Russian strat-session firms, Vistage/EO) announcing AI-augmented formats. This may reflect the limited search budget, not absence.
- No sizing of the specific segment (solo founders / tiny teams who already use Claude/ChatGPT for strategy) was possible from available sources.
